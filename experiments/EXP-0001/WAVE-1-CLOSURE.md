## Scope

Wave 1 investigated: **What is required to sustain one human being for 24 hours?**

The wave began from biology and physical dependency rather than prices.

## Canonical result

Scientific Core v0.1 establishes a typed dependency architecture based on stocks, flows, bounded states, processes, capacities, environmental conditions, and external mediation.

Two governing distinctions remain central:

- **Requirement != Consumption != Replenishment**
- **24-hour observation window != replenishment horizon**

The wave also established **Inventory Blindness** as the error of inferring same-window external replenishment directly from observed use while ignoring initial stocks, internal production, recycling, and conditional reserves.

Wave 1 does **not** establish a universal daily survival bundle, a universal reference human, or the monetary cost of one human day.

# EXP-0001 Research Questions

1. What must enter the human system?
2. What must leave it?
3. What must continuously remain within viable ranges?
4. Which requirements are continuous?
5. Which are intermittent?
6. Which can be stored?
7. Which require external infrastructure?
8. Which are consumed directly?
9. Which are enabling conditions rather than consumable inputs?
10. Which requirements vary materially by age, sex, body size, activity, climate, health, geography, altitude, pregnancy/lactation, or other variables?
11. Where is a universal number impossible or misleading?
12. For each dependency, what is the distinction among requirement, consumption, and replenishment?
13. What is its physiological replenishment horizon?
14. Where evidence permits, what thresholds affect survival, stability, function, long-term health, and comfort?

# EXP-0001 Boundary

A living human organism at t0 is assumed to have preexisting internal reserves and functioning baseline physiological systems.

Inside the experiment boundary at t0 may include body water, glycogen, adipose tissue, vitamin/mineral stores, tissue integrity, cardiovascular capacity, respiratory capacity, renal capacity, thermoregulatory capacity, immune capacity, accumulated sleep state, and other physiological stocks.

These are initial conditions, not zero-cost lifetime resources.

The experiment asks what must remain true during the next 24 hours for continued viability and explicitly defined higher functional states.

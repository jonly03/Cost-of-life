# Governance

## Authority

The human Principal Investigator (PI) is final authority for consequential project decisions.

The Copilot / Research Director is the primary integration authority beneath that threshold.

## Desk branches

- `research/biology`
- `research/economics`
- `research/data-modeling`
- `research/library`
- `research/red-team`
- `publication/editorial`
- `design/visualization`
- `development/peom`

Branches are epistemic sandboxes owned by functions, not ephemeral agent instances.

## Meaning of main

`main` is canonical integrated knowledge, not a dumping ground.

Promotion requires, where applicable:

1. persistent identifier;
2. provenance;
3. evidence attached to consequential claims;
4. Librarian verification of source attribution;
5. Red Team review of material inference/model consequences;
6. unresolved uncertainty preserved explicitly;
7. cross-desk contradictions resolved or documented;
8. Director integration approval;
9. PI approval for consequential scope, methodology, contested interpretation, publication conclusions, or PEOM architecture changes.

"Validated-for-model-use" means accepted within a stated scope and model version, not universally proven.

## Research promotion lifecycle

Explore → Document → Challenge → Revise → Accept → Integrate

## Identifier policy

Identifiers are persistent and never recycled. Rejected or superseded objects retain their IDs and history.

Prefixes:

- EXP — experiment
- BIO — biology claim
- ECO — economics claim
- DATA — data/modeling claim or specification
- REF — source
- RT — Red Team challenge
- TERM — terminology
- PEOM — PEOM model/component
- VIS — visualization
- PUB — publication
- RAD — Research Architecture Decision

# RAD-0001 — Version-Controlled Evidence Architecture

Status: **Accepted**

## Context

The Cost of Life is both an interdisciplinary research project and a meta-experiment in human-directed agentic research. Chat-based specialist work is transient and difficult to audit across time.

## Decision

Adopt Git as the lab's durable research substrate.

Treat experiments, claims, sources, challenges, models, terminology, publications, visualizations, and methodological decisions as version-controlled research objects.

Add a dedicated Librarian / Reference & Evidence Desk responsible for source discovery, characterization, evidence mapping, citation audit, and corpus management.

Separate Librarian provenance review from Red Team inference review.

Use desk-owned branches as epistemic sandboxes and `main` as canonical integrated knowledge.

Adopt: Explore → Document → Challenge → Revise → Accept → Integrate.

Treat research pull requests as propositions to modify canonical knowledge state.

## Decision drivers

Reproducibility; evidence provenance; agent coordination; recoverability across sessions; explicit disagreement; model traceability; eventual machine-readable knowledge graph.

## Consequences

Benefits include traceable claims, durable disagreement, preserved research history, and backward traceability from publications/PEOM calculations to assumptions and sources.

Costs include identifier management, review overhead, process discipline, and expected schema evolution.

## Rejected alternative

Use chats and reports as the primary research record and reconstruct provenance later.

Reason: unacceptable provenance debt.

## Principle

We are not merely preserving conclusions. We are preserving the path by which conclusions became admissible.

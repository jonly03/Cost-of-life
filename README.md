# The Cost of Life

## The Micro and Macro Economics of a Human Life

This repository is the durable research substrate for **The Cost of Life — Agentic Research Lab**.

Core question:

> What does it actually cost for a human being to exist?

Research begins below money: physical and biochemical requirements, then resource requirements, delivery mechanisms, infrastructure, economic goods and services, and only then price.

## Canonical state

`main` represents the lab's current best integrated, auditable understanding. Exploratory work belongs on desk-owned branches.

# VIS-0001 Revision History

## RT-0009 — sleep/recovery outcome-layer encoding

**Status:** correction applied; mechanical Red Team re-check requested  
**Scope:** primary diagram sleep/recovery process capsule only

### Previous encoding

`sleep / recovery` used the same filled process capsule treatment as ATP/metabolism, circulation, gas exchange, and renal processing.

### Risk

Visual parity could imply that Scientific Core v0.1 established sleep/recovery as a coequal acute 24-hour viability process.

### Correction

Sleep/recovery remains inside the process grammar, but its capsule now uses a visually differentiated dashed/neutral treatment and the explicit qualifier:

**function / health**

No quantity or sleep-duration recommendation is shown. No acute survival threshold is asserted.

### Canonical basis

- **BIO-0013:** sleep is important for cognitive performance and long-term health; acute sleep loss can impair function; no universal 24-hour survival minimum is established.
- **DATA-0008:** sleep constraints concern function/long-term health here, not a demonstrated 24-hour survival minimum; quantitative sleep-state dynamics remain unresolved.
- **RT-0009:** accepted by Research Director; public translation must preserve the outcome-layer distinction.

### Re-check gate

Request a **mechanical Red Team re-check of the changed sleep/recovery visual element only**. No broader VIS-0001 reopening is requested.
